# Swiggy Customer Retention Analysis

Customer retention analysis conducted as part of a Product Management Case Competition, using Excel and PowerPoint to develop a data-driven product strategy for Swiggy.


## Problem Statement

Swiggy faces a significant retention challenge: with a 36.88% churn rate across 5,000 customers and 61.7% of orders delayed, the platform is losing both customers and revenue. The goal was to identify root causes of churn, segment customers by value and risk, and recommend product solutions to increase repeat orders and customer lifetime value (CLV).

**Tools:** Excel · PowerPoint


## Analysis Performed

- **Customer Segmentation**  Categorized 5,000 customers into three segments: High Value High Frequency (1,262), Loyal but Price Sensitive (1,673), and At-Risk High Value Customers (2,065)
- **Churn & Retention Analysis**  Identified churn drivers including delivery delays, order cancellations, and low app engagement
- **Delivery Performance Analysis**  Investigated ETA accuracy, delay frequency, and their correlation with churn
- **Root Cause Analysis (5 Whys)**  Traced high delivery delays to the absence of a dynamic ETA engine integrating real-time traffic, kitchen load, and partner availability
- **Value vs. Effort Prioritization**  Mapped solutions across a 2×2 matrix to identify quick wins vs. strategic initiatives
- **KPI Baseline & Product Roadmap**  Defined Tier 1 and Tier 2 KPIs and a 12-month quarterly rollout plan


## Key Insights

1. **Delivery delays are the strongest controllable churn driver**  Customers who experienced delays churned at 48.64% vs. 18.75% for on-time customers, a 29.89 percentage point gap.
2. **Cancellations create a high-risk customer pool**  70% of customers who cancelled and did not reorder churned, with 431 cancellations putting ₹1.5L in revenue at risk.
3. **Low app engagement strongly predicts churn**  Customers with ≤5 app-usage days churned at 51.88% vs. 27.90% for those with >15 days, highlighting engagement as a key retention lever.


## Recommendations

1. **Deploy a Dynamic ETA Engine**  Integrate real-time traffic, kitchen load, and partner GPS data to continuously update delivery ETAs, targeting a reduction in delayed orders from 61.7% to 47.9% and saving ~206 customers from churn in Q2.
2. **Implement a Smart Restaurant SLA Management System**  Automate order rerouting for unaccepted orders after 5 minutes, penalize repeat SLA violators with search demotion, and reward compliant merchants, targeting a 35% reduction in cancellation rate.
3. **Build a Loyalty Program Beyond Coupon Dependency**  Reduce coupon dependency by 15% through engagement-based rewards, saving ~₹32,683 in discount costs in Q3 while increasing CLV from ₹399.74 to ₹499.68 by Q4.
